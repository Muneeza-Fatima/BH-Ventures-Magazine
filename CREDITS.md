# Photography credits

Every photograph in this magazine is used under a licence that permits
**commercial use and derivative works**. Both are required: the book is
printed and sold as corporate communication, and every frame is cropped and
colour graded before it reaches a page.

CC BY-SA, CC BY-ND and CC BY-NC are excluded on purpose. The filter lives in
`build/fetch-images.mjs`, and it reads the licence out of the file's
**wikitext**, not out of the API's `license` field — Commons reports every file
page as CC BY-SA 4.0 because that is the licence of the page text, not of the
picture on it. Trusting that field would have rejected both photographs below.

## Withdrawn, and why

Three photographs were removed rather than used. All three are in
`assets/images/retired/` so the decisions can be re-examined rather than
re-made.

| File | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `cover-dubai-day` (Dubai Marina, golden hour) | A tower mid-frame carries **"DAMAC"** in legible type. Another developer's brand across the front of a BH Ventures magazine implies a relationship that does not exist, and the sign cannot be cropped out — it sits in the middle of the composition. |
| `cover-dubai-skyline` (Business Bay, night) | The client read it as Doha rather than Dubai. Whether or not that is fair, a cover photograph that has to be defended has already failed. |
| `ventures-automobile` (car carrier at sea), `ventures-foodstuff` (forklift), `ventures-workspace` (atrium) | Right category, wrong subject. The brief is that every venture picture is the **literal** subject of its own service — cars for automobile export, dates for foodstuff. A ship is not a car and a forklift is not food. |

The cover frame that replaced them had the same fault and survived it: the
right-hand tower in `Burj Khalifa Night View 02` carries **"Fakhruddin"** in
lit type. It is cropped out in `build/grade-images.mjs`, which takes 2500 px of
the 3024 available from the left edge. That still prints at 294 dpi.
